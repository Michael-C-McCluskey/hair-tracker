Install on your phone

In Chrome, open the menu and tap Install app or Add to Home screen. Hair Track works offline after that. It needs to be served over HTTPS (GitHub Pages works).

About

Hair Track keeps every photo in IndexedDB on this device. There is no account and no cloud upload. Clearing this site’s data in your browser will delete your photos — export a backup first.
